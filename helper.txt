Metrics: 

Retrieval precision:
fraction of retrieved chunks whose text contains at least one expected keyword or expected section marker.
Context recall:
fraction of expected keywords found across retrieved context.
Section hit rate:
fraction of expected sections found in retrieved context text.
Latency:
per-question latency and aggregate average/p95.
Cache hit rate:
count cache_hit=True across eval samples.
Answer metrics:
only when --generate-answers is enabled.
use existing RAGEvaluator.evaluate(...) for answer relevance, faithfulness, hallucination label, and context score.
